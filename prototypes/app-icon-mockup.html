Inbetween icon directions

Round two. A and B are your picks, kept as the reference. M1 to M4 mix them: the ring, in-between dot and solid dot from A, set on the triangle mesh from B. Each is shown at 240 px, then at true pixel size (128, 64, 32, 16) on a light and a dark strip.

Proposed source and sizes

Whichever direction wins, the answer to the ticket would be: one hand-written SVG as the source of truth, rasterised by a small xtask into every platform format and checked in, so building needs no extra tools.
